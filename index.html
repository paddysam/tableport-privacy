Tableport Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-27

Tableport is a browser extension that exports tables from the web page you are viewing to CSV, Excel, Markdown, JSON or your clipboard.

What Tableport does with your data

  • Tableport reads the tables on the current page only when you open its popup (via the browser's "activeTab" permission).
  • Table contents are processed entirely inside your browser. Tableport does not send page content, tables, URLs or browsing history to us or to anyone else.
  • Your settings and the "Basket" of tables you chose to collect are saved in your browser's local extension storage on your device. You can clear them with the Basket's "Clear" button or by removing the extension.
  • Tableport has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no remote code.

Payments (only if a paid "Pro" edition is ever published)

Paid features are sold through ExtensionPay (extensionpay.com), which uses Stripe to process payments. When you upgrade or log in, ExtensionPay and Stripe collect the information needed for the purchase (such as your email address and payment details) on their own pages under their own privacy policies. Tableport never sees your card details. The extension only receives whether your account is paid or in trial so it can unlock Pro features, and stores an anonymous identifier assigned by ExtensionPay in the browser's extension storage.

The free version of Tableport contains no payment code and makes no network requests.

Sharing

We do not sell or share user data. We do not use data for any purpose unrelated to Tableport's single purpose.

Children

Tableport is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this URL with a new date.
